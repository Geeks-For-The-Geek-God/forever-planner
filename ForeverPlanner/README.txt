Forever Planner 0.4.0 (WoW: Forever, interface 16001)

INSTALL
1. Close the game.
2. Unzip so the folder sits at:  World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns\ForeverPlanner
   (use the same AddOns folder that holds Questie).
3. Start the game. Questie and TomTom are optional but recommended.

USE
- The tracker shows your current route step, its quests and what to do next.
  It ticks itself off from your quest log and advances on its own.
- Buttons: < > browse, Now returns to your step, Done/Skip mark a step (again to undo),
  Way sets a waypoint, Plan opens the window (Route, Talents, Macros, Gear). Right-click the tracker also opens it.
- /fp catchup: if you skipped quests earlier on the route, this previews skipping those steps so the
  tracker starts where you really are. /fp catchup confirm applies it; /fp catchup undo reverses it.
- Gear: the tracker shows planned items on the step you're on. The Gear tab lists what you still need
  in route order (click one for its step and a waypoint). You get a prompt when a planned item lands
  in your bags, and the quest reward window names the reward your plan wants.
- /fp help lists every command. /fp check explains why a step isn't ticking.
- Key bindings: Esc > Key Bindings > AddOns > Forever Planner.

If something errors, type /console scriptErrors 1, reproduce it, and paste the error back into the Druid chat.
