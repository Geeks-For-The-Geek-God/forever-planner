## Forever Planner 0.5.0: a plan for every character

**Restart the game once after this update** (it adds new files; `/reload` is enough after that).

- **Every character, every class, both factions.** On first login you get a levelling route from your race's starting zone to 60 (Durotar, Mulgore, Tirisfal, Zephras Isle, Elwynn, Dun Morogh, Teldrassil and on), with each step's quests matched by quest ID.
- **One-time setup window:** pick your spec (the recommended one is ticked) and whether to include group quests. `/fp setup` changes it.
- **Talents for all nine classes** (ForeverDB's levelling builds), with the next pick glowing on the talent window as before.
- **The tracker now follows places too:** "go here" points tick when you arrive, trainer and flight-path visits tick when the window opens, level goals tick on level-up. The waypoint moves to the next goal by itself.
- **Class quests** (pets, forms, class items): the tracker offers them when you reach their level. Click to add one before your current step; `/fp side` lists them.
- **Warlock plan:** macros for five keys (ATTACK, DOTS, DRAIN, PET, SELF) for Affliction, Demonology and Destruction, trainer notes and a levelling gear list.
- **Druid:** your own route still runs from level 21; new Druids follow Mulgore, Silverpine and the Barrens before it. Existing characters are caught up automatically (`/fp catchup undo` reverses it).
- Routes and talent builds come from Polestar (GPL-3.0); Forever Planner is now GPL-3.0 too. See NOTICE.md.
