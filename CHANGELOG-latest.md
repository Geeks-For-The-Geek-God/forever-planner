## Forever Planner 0.6.4: gear panel clear of the side tabs

`/reload` after the updater installs it.

- The gear panel no longer sits under the character window's side tabs (your portrait and the other tab icons on the window's right edge). It now finds where they end and docks just past them. The "<" band button they used to cover is visible again.
- The Show/Hide gear plan button is below the character window, so it's on screen even when the window is at the top of your screen.
- The header shows just the two tabs, without the spec name that was being cut off. Hover the Gear plan tab to see your spec.
- The close button sits inside the panel's corner.
- `/fp geardebug` also reports how far right of the character window the panel sits.
