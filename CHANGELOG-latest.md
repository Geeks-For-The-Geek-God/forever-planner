## Forever Planner 0.5.3

`/reload` after the updater installs it.

- **Class quests you already have are no longer offered.** The tracker's "Class quest ready" line now checks whether you already know the spell the quest teaches (Cure Poison, Bear Form, pets and so on) or own its reward item. It also counts the chain as done once you've handed in its last quest for your own race and faction. Before, it also waited for the other faction's version of the quest, so the offer never went away.
- A class quest that's already on your route isn't offered separately, unless that route step was skipped.
