## Forever Planner 0.6.5: the Priest plan

`/reload` after the updater installs it.

- **Priest:** the plan now comes from the Priest brief, the same way the Druid's does:
  - **Talents:** Discipline wand 23/0/7 for levels 10-39, in the brief's order (Wand Specialization, Spirit Tap, Twin Disciplines…). Your talent window shows the hint and glow for the next pick. Before, it pointed at a generic Discipline build (Power in Light first) that your Priest wasn't following.
  - **Respec at 40:** the tracker, the talent window and a level-up message tell you to reset your talents at the trainer. After that, the hint follows Shadow 17/0/34 (31 Shadow points for Shadowform, then Discipline).
  - **Macros:** the five keys from the brief (ATTACK, DEFEND, BUFF, RECOVER, PANIC), changing at 12, 16, 35 and 40.
  - **Trainer reminders** for every level with new spells, and the brief's wand path as stopgaps.
  - **Gear:** Discipline picks to 39, Shadow picks from 40, in the gear route, the gear panel and Craft now.
  - A second choice in the setup window, "Shadow from 10", for going Shadow without a respec.
- Engine: class files can give talent phases (a respec at a level) and switch the gear spec by level.
